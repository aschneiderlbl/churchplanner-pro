Concept prototype

Finish drafts by talking.

Grit attaches each spoken note to the exact text it concerns, then works through your notes in a queue while you keep going.

  1. Tap a sentence, then hold the mic.
  2. Open Listen, press play and hold to talk.
  3. Check the Queue to review or undo changes.

Nothing records your voice or calls a model. Notes, edits and answers are scripted. Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in voice.
