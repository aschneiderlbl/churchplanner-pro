A person, with software doing the routine work. Confirmations and reminders go out on a schedule we set up together. Anything unusual, like a sick volunteer at 7 AM, comes to me.

However you keep it now: Planning Center, a Google Sheet, a spreadsheet, or a photo of the sign-up sheet in the lobby.
