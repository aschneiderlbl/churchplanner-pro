Good questions

Is Andrew a person or software?

A person, with software doing the heavy lifting. Routine confirmations and reminders go out on a schedule I set up with you. Anything unusual, like a sick volunteer at 7 AM, comes to me.

How do I get you our schedule?

However you keep it now: Planning Center, a Google Sheet, a spreadsheet, or a photo of the paper sign-up sheet in the lobby.

Do my volunteers need an app or account?

No. They get texts from your church’s number and reply. That’s it.

Can you join our staff group chat?

Yes. Add my number to the group like any other contact and ask me there: “Who’s on nursery Sunday?”

What happens to our volunteers’ information?

It’s used only to schedule your church’s volunteers. It is never sold or used for marketing. Security details
