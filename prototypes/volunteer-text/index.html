Questions

Do volunteers need an app?

No. They get texts and reply, on any phone, including a flip phone.

Will volunteers know Andrew is AI?

Yes. The first time Andrew texts a volunteer, he introduces himself as your church’s AI assistant.

Does anything go out without my OK?

Confirmations and reminders go out on a schedule you set up together. Anything unusual, like a sick volunteer at 7 AM, comes to you.

What happens to our volunteers’ information?

It’s used only to schedule your church’s volunteers, never sold and never used for marketing. How we keep it safe

How do I stop?

Remove Andrew from the thread, or just tell him to stop texting you. Volunteers can do the same anytime.
