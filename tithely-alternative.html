Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Church software can become complicated quickly.

More products. More settings. More functionality. More things for your staff to learn and manage.

ChurchPlannerPro is intentionally different.

We focus on the everyday operations that smaller churches actually need to run:

  • Keep member records accurate
  • Accept and track giving
  • Check kids into programs
  • Fill volunteer shifts
  • Run Sunday school and VBS
  • Communicate with members
  • Manage church finances
  • Keep track of facilities
  • Respond to pastoral care needs
  • Give members a simple church app
  • Know what needs your attention

Just what you need. No extra bells and whistles.

That means we aren't trying to build a website builder, worship planning platform, sermon hosting service, or every other piece of technology a church might possibly use.

We're focused on one thing:

Automating your church operations.

Built for smaller churches with limited staff

Large churches can have dedicated staff for finance, communications, children's ministry, facilities, volunteer coordination, and administration.

Smaller churches don't have that luxury.

One person might be updating member records in the morning, organizing Sunday school after lunch, finding someone to fill a volunteer opening, answering a member question, reconciling donations, and sending the church newsletter before going home.

That's the church ChurchPlannerPro is built for.

Instead of giving your staff more software to manage, ChurchPlannerPro helps automate the work they're already doing.

Automate the work behind your church

Most church software helps you store information.

ChurchPlannerPro is designed to help you do something with it.

A family registers for Sunday school

Their information flows into the program so administrators can manage registration, rooms, check-in, and attendance without maintaining another spreadsheet.

A volunteer can't serve Sunday

Volunteers can pick up, swap, or drop shifts while administrators maintain visibility into the schedule.

Someone gives to your church

Giving through Stripe flows into ChurchPlannerPro so your church can track donations and maintain accurate giving records.

A member submits a form

Their record can be updated and the appropriate workflow can begin automatically.

Something needs your attention

ChurchPlannerPro helps surface what needs to get done instead of relying on an administrator to remember everything happening across the church.

The goal isn't another system your church has to manage.

The goal is less work for your staff.

Focus on outcomes, not features

Your church doesn't need volunteer scheduling software because volunteer scheduling is exciting.

You need people to show up on Sunday.

You don't need registration software because you want another database.

You need Sunday school and VBS to run smoothly.

That's why ChurchPlannerPro is built around the outcomes your church is responsible for:

Every volunteer shift is filled

Publish schedules and let volunteers pick up, swap, and drop shifts while your team stays in control.

Sunday school and VBS run smoothly

Manage registration, room assignments, check-in, and weekly attendance in one place.

Financials reconcile every month

Bring financial information together and make it easier to keep your church's records accurate.

Accurate giving statements are ready on time

Track giving throughout the year so you're not scrambling when it's time to prepare statements.

Members know what's happening

Keep your congregation informed through church communications and your member app.

Stay on budget

Give your team better visibility into church finances throughout the year.

Member needs get answered quickly

Track pastoral care and other member needs so requests don't get lost in someone's inbox or memory.

Know what needs your attention

Bring the important work happening across your church into one place.
