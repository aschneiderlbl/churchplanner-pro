ChurchPlannerProvs.Vanco

A Vanco alternative for running your entire church operation

ChurchPlannerPro vs. Vanco: how to choose the right platform for your church's giving, operations and administrative work.

Vanco has served churches for decades and is especially well known for electronic giving and payment processing.

Today, Vanco offers churches tools for online giving, mobile giving, text giving, donor engagement, events, communications and more.

For churches primarily looking for a proven digital giving platform, Vanco can be a strong option.

But collecting donations is only one part of running a church.

Someone still needs to fill volunteer shifts. Register children for Sunday school. Coordinate VBS. Communicate with members. Reconcile financials. Maintain member records. Follow up on requests. Manage forms. Track the budget. And figure out what needs attention next.

ChurchPlannerPro is built around that work.

Instead of starting with giving and adding additional tools around it, ChurchPlannerPro starts with a different question:

What does your church need to get done?

Then we connect your systems, manage the work in one place and automate as much of the follow-up as possible.

Start with the work your church needs to get done

Church administrators rarely start their morning thinking:

"I need more software features."

They're thinking:

  • Are all of Sunday's volunteer shifts filled?
  • Is Sunday school ready?
  • Did everyone register for VBS get assigned correctly?
  • Do our financials reconcile?
  • Are our member records accurate?
  • Did we follow up with that family?
  • Does everyone know what's happening this week?
  • What am I forgetting?

ChurchPlannerPro is organized around answering those questions.

ChurchPlannerPro outcomes

The things your church actually needs to get done.

  • Every volunteer shift is filled
  • Sunday school and VBS run smoothly
  • Financials reconcile every month
  • Accurate giving statements are ready on time
  • Members know what's happening
  • Stay on budget
  • Member needs get answered quickly
  • Know what needs your attention

Instead of giving your staff another system to manage, the goal is to automate the repetitive work behind these outcomes.

Giving is important. But it's only one workflow.

Vanco's roots are in giving and payment processing.

Churches can use Vanco to accept gifts online, through mobile devices and through text. Vanco also provides donor reporting, engagement tools and integrations with church management systems.

That's valuable infrastructure.

But after a donation comes in, your church still has work to do.

The gift may need to be associated with the right person and fund. Financial records need to be reconciled. Giving statements need to be prepared. Budgets need to be monitored. Member information needs to stay accurate.

ChurchPlannerPro looks at that larger workflow.

Giving → Records → Reconciliation → Reporting → Giving statements

Instead of treating the transaction as the end of the process, ChurchPlannerPro helps organize and automate the operational work around it.

Fill the volunteer schedule without managing another spreadsheet

A volunteer schedule isn't just a calendar.

Someone needs to determine what positions are needed, publish the schedule, find volunteers, handle changes and remind everyone when they're serving.

ChurchPlannerPro brings that workflow together.

Administrators publish the shifts that need to be filled.

Members can see open opportunities in the member app and pick up a shift.

If their plans change, they can swap or drop their shift.

Automatic reminders help everyone know where they need to be.

Publish schedule → Volunteers pick up shifts → Automatic reminders

Your staff can focus on the remaining gaps instead of coordinating everything through spreadsheets, SignUpGenius and email.

Run Sunday school and VBS as a program, not a collection of tools

Church programs create a surprising amount of administrative work.

Take Sunday school.

You may need to:

  • Register children
  • Maintain family information
  • Assign children to classrooms
  • Schedule teachers and volunteers
  • Record attendance
  • Communicate with parents
  • Manage forms
  • Follow up when information is missing

Those aren't separate problems.

They're all part of running Sunday school.

ChurchPlannerPro brings them together around the program.

The same approach can be used for VBS, confirmation, Bible studies and other recurring church programs.

Keep members informed without rebuilding your audience every time

Church communication depends on good member information.

When your people, programs, groups and communications are connected, your staff doesn't have to maintain separate lists every time something changes.

ChurchPlannerPro helps churches keep member information organized and use that information to communicate with the right people.

That means less time managing lists and more confidence that members know what's happening.

You don't have to replace Vanco

Here's an important distinction:

ChurchPlannerPro can work with Vanco.

If your church already uses Vanco for giving and it works well, you don't necessarily need to replace it.

Vanco can continue handling the giving experience and payment processing while ChurchPlannerPro helps automate the operational work surrounding it.

That reflects the broader ChurchPlannerPro philosophy:

Keep the systems that work. Replace the ones that don't. Connect everything together.

Your church shouldn't have to go through a massive software replacement project just to improve its operations.

ChurchPlannerPro is designed to work with the systems your church already runs and help connect the work happening between them.

You shouldn't have to implement church software by yourself

Changing church software is rarely just a technology project.

Member records need to be migrated.

Duplicates need to be resolved.

Old information needs to be cleaned up.

Financial information needs to be checked.

Processes need to be configured.

Staff and volunteers need to understand what changes.

ChurchPlannerPro includes hands-on implementation.

We work alongside your church to:

  • Understand how your church currently operates
  • Connect the systems you want to keep
  • Migrate information from systems you're replacing
  • Audit and clean your data
  • Configure ChurchPlannerPro around your workflows
  • Help your team get started

For a smaller church without dedicated IT or operations staff, you shouldn't have to figure all of that out yourself.

No admin? No problem.

Sometimes software isn't the entire problem.

Your church simply doesn't have enough administrative time.

A pastor, volunteer or part-time employee may be trying to maintain member records, prepare communications, coordinate volunteers, manage programs and reconcile information between systems.

ChurchPlannerPro also offers an optional part-time church administrator for churches that need ongoing help.

That administrator can help with work such as:

  • Keeping member records updated
  • Managing events and registrations
  • Coordinating volunteers
  • Preparing communications and newsletters
  • Managing forms and follow-up
  • Running reports and reconciliation
  • Migrating and cleaning data
  • Keeping systems in sync
  • Handling recurring administrative tasks

The administrator service is separate from the $9.99/month ChurchPlannerPro subscription.

In fairness, Vanco has real advantages

Vanco has been serving churches for decades and has built significant expertise around electronic giving.

It supports online, mobile and text giving, provides implementation support and integrates with more than 60 church management systems.

If your primary goal is improving how members donate to your church, Vanco deserves consideration.

And if your church already uses Vanco successfully, ChurchPlannerPro isn't necessarily asking you to change that.

The question is what happens around your giving system.

If Vanco handles your donations but your staff still relies on spreadsheets, SignUpGenius, disconnected databases, email threads and manual follow-up to run the rest of the church, that's where ChurchPlannerPro is designed to help.

So, is ChurchPlannerPro a good Vanco alternative?

It depends on what you're trying to accomplish.

Vanco may fit your church if:

  • Your primary need is online and electronic giving.
  • You want mobile and text-to-give options.
  • You want an established payment processor with decades of church experience.
  • Your existing church management system already handles the rest of your operations well.
  • You primarily need a giving platform that integrates with your existing ChMS.

ChurchPlannerPro may fit your church if:

  • You want to automate your church operations, not just giving.
  • Your church is smaller and has a lean administrative team.
  • You want volunteer scheduling, programs, communication, financial workflows and member information connected.
  • You want one platform for $9.99/month.
  • You want hands-on implementation, migration and data cleanup included.
  • You want to keep Vanco for giving while automating the work around it.
  • You may benefit from an optional part-time church administrator.

The platforms don't necessarily have to compete.

For some churches, Vanco + ChurchPlannerPro may actually be the right combination.

Vanco can process the gifts.

ChurchPlannerPro can help automate the church operations around them.
