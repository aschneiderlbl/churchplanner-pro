Shepherd’s Staff Alternative: ChurchPlannerPro for Churches Ready to Work Online

Shepherd’s Staff is a capable church recordkeeping system. It handles membership, attendance, contributions, scheduling, and, with the Finance edition, church accounting. For a church whose office runs on Windows PCs and needs detailed records and reports, those are real strengths. [1]

But church work rarely stays at the office computer. Volunteers need to see open shifts. Parents need to register children. Staff need to follow up, send updates, and keep information moving between people and systems.

ChurchPlannerPro is an alternative for smaller churches that want to automate those day-to-day operations in a web-based platform. It brings member records, volunteer scheduling, programs, giving, communications, forms, and follow-up into one place. [2]

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

Shepherd’s Staff’s Scheduler does schedule volunteers, WebTools lets people enter certain information through a browser, and child check-in includes barcode name tags and visitor features. The question is which workflow your church wants at the center of its day. [1, 3]

Where Shepherd’s Staff is stronger

Detailed, established recordkeeping. Its dedicated modules cover membership, attendance, contributions, finance, and scheduling. Contribution tools cover pledges and statements, while attendance tools help staff spot patterns and absences. [1, 3]

Accounting inside the software. Churches that want funds, accounts, vendors, checks, deposits, and journal entries in their church management system should examine Shepherd’s Staff with Finance.

ChurchPlannerPro connects church operations with accounting tools such as QuickBooks. Map existing finance tasks before changing systems. [1, 2]

A dedicated check-in setup. Shepherd’s Staff offers child check-in with barcode name tags, allergy indicators, and kiosk options. Evaluate that setup closely if your church relies on it. [3]

Where ChurchPlannerPro fits better

Volunteer scheduling that members can act on. An administrator can publish shifts, and members can pick up, drop, or swap them. Shepherd’s Staff also schedules volunteers and staff; compare how each product handles open shifts and midweek changes. [3]

Programs, communication, and follow-up in one workflow. A Sunday school registration leads to records, check-in, volunteer coordination, and perhaps a follow-up. ChurchPlannerPro connects those steps with forms, programs, communication, and workflows. [2]

Access beyond one PC. Shepherd’s Staff calls its core product PC-based. Its separate WebTools portal supports selected browser tasks and syncs with the data on the church computer. ChurchPlannerPro is designed as a web-based platform for staff and member workflows. [1, 3]

What does each one cost?

Shepherd’s Staff lists a one-time purchase price of $599 to $1,199, depending on church size and Finance. The first year of support is included. Listed annual support prices afterward range from $375 to $625; support covers upgrades, backups, training, and WebTools. Confirm your edition and costs with the vendor. [4]

ChurchPlannerPro currently advertises $9.99 per month for the whole platform, with a 90-day free period and invite-based onboarding. Its site says the price is 50% off for the first year, so ask what the price will be afterward. [2]

Which should your church choose?

Choose Shepherd’s Staff if detailed church records and reports, accounting in the same PC-based system, or an established process built around its modules are your priorities.

Consider ChurchPlannerPro if your church has a small administrative team and wants staff, members, and volunteers to handle more of the week’s work together online. It is especially worth a look when open shifts, registrations, communications, and follow-up take the most time.

Bring one real process to a demo: a volunteer cancellation, Sunday school registration, or month-end finance task. Ask who does each step, where the information goes, and what the administrator still does manually.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shepherd’s Staff cloud-based? Its main software is PC-based. With support, WebTools offers browser access to selected tasks, including attendance and offering entry. [1, 3, 4]

Does Shepherd’s Staff have volunteer scheduling? Yes. Its Scheduler schedules volunteers and staff as well as rooms and resources. Explore how volunteers participate in filling and changing their shifts. [3]

Does ChurchPlannerPro replace Shepherd’s Staff Finance? ChurchPlannerPro offers giving and financial workflows and connects with QuickBooks. Map funds, accounts, checks, and journal entries before moving an existing accounting process. [1, 2]

Can a small church use either product? Yes. Shepherd’s Staff offers a Small Church Edition for 1–149 households. ChurchPlannerPro focuses on coordinating operations with fewer dedicated staff. [2, 4]
