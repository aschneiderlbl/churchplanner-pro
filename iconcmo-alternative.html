Accounting is one of the biggest differences between ChurchPlannerPro and IconCMO.

IconCMO: Church accounting is built directly into the platform.

ChurchPlannerPro: We integrate with QuickBooks, so your church can keep the accounting system it already knows while ChurchPlannerPro handles your church operations around it.

That means you don't have to move your accounting system just because you want better church management software.

If your church wants to replace QuickBooks with dedicated church fund-accounting software, IconCMO's approach may make sense.

But if QuickBooks already works for your church, why replace it?

Keep QuickBooks for accounting. Use ChurchPlannerPro to automate your church operations.

Where IconCMO stands out: built-in church accounting

IconCMO takes a different approach to accounting.

Rather than connecting church management software with a separate accounting platform like QuickBooks, IconCMO offers church-specific fund accounting within its product ecosystem.

That can be valuable for churches that want to bring their accounting and church management into the same system.

IconCMO's accounting capabilities include tools for areas such as:

  • Fund accounting
  • General ledger
  • Banking
  • Budgeting
  • Financial reporting
  • Accounts payable and receivable on applicable plans
  • Payroll options

If replacing QuickBooks with dedicated church accounting software is one of your main goals, IconCMO deserves consideration.

ChurchPlannerPro isn't trying to recreate an accounting system that millions of businesses and organizations already use.

We integrate with QuickBooks instead.

For many smaller churches, that's the simpler approach.
