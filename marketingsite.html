Questions church offices actually ask

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?
No. ChurchPlannerPro connects to the systems your church already runs — your people database, giving platform, accounting, calendar, and email. Keep the tools you love. Replace the ones you don't. Most churches start by connecting what they have and automating one workflow.
What exactly counts as an "automation"?
A trigger and the steps that follow it. Something happens — a form is submitted, a visitor attends, an event gets close, a task goes past due — and ChurchPlannerPro carries out the next actions: updating records, sending messages, assigning tasks, notifying the right person. You can see every step of every run.
How is this different from choosing modules on another church platform?
Modules are parts. Outcomes are what your church actually needs. Most platforms sell you Registrations, Check-ins, People and Publishing and leave you to carry a child's name from one to the next — that carrying is the work. ChurchPlannerPro is organized around the work your office actually does, and the carrying between the steps is the product, not your office manager's Thursday night.
Who sets the automations up?
You do, in plain language — "every Monday, find first-time visitors from Sunday who haven't been contacted and assign them to Pastor Mike." Nothing to script. We also ship a set of common church workflows you can turn on as-is and adjust later.
What if an automation does the wrong thing?
Every automation shows its full run history — what triggered it, what it did, and when. You can pause any automation, require a person to approve a step before it runs, or test one on a single record before turning it on for the whole church.
How long does setup take?
You can start in an afternoon: connect one system, import your people, and turn on a single automation the same day. Most churches start with visitor follow-up, because it is the one that hurts when it slips. Moving everything else across happens at whatever pace suits your office — there is no cutover date.
Who is ChurchPlannerPro for?
The people who make everything run: church administrators, executive pastors, operations directors, office managers, senior pastors, ministry leaders, volunteer coordinators, and church tech leaders — anyone who knows how much work happens behind the scenes every week.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to leave our current church management system?
No. Connect it and let ChurchPlannerPro handle what happens around it. Plenty of churches keep their people database exactly where it is and use ChurchPlannerPro for the parts it does not cover — volunteers, facilities, reconciliation, Sunday school — and for the automation that ties them together.
Is automation appropriate for pastoral work?
Automation should never send a pastoral message someone did not intend. What it should do is make sure the right person is told, with the right context, at the right time — and then track whether the visit or call actually happened. Any step can require a human approval before it runs, and care data is visible only to the roles you allow.
What happens when an automation gets it wrong?
You will see it, because every run is logged step by step. Pause the automation, fix the rule, and re-run it against a single record before turning it back on. Nothing runs silently and nothing runs that you have not switched on.
We are a small church with one part-time administrator. Is this overkill?
That is the case where it matters most. A large staff can absorb a dropped handoff; a one-person office cannot. Small churches usually start with two automations — visitor follow-up and the Monday brief — and add more as they see them working.
How much of our data has to move before this is useful?
One system and one automation. Connect your people data, turn on visitor follow-up, and you have removed a weekly chore by the end of the day. Everything after that is your call, at your pace.
What does it cost?
ChurchPlannerPro is in early access with partner churches while we build it against real church operations. Talk to us about what your church needs and we will be straightforward about pricing and timelines.
