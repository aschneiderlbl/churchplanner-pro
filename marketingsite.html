About

Built for church admins

I started ChurchPlannerPro after seeing church administrators do incredible work with limited time and resources, often holding everything together with spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected tools.

Church administrators deserve great software built around the outcomes they are responsible for: filling volunteer shifts, running church programs, reconciling finances, communicating with members, and knowing what needs their attention.

After more than a decade of building software, I created ChurchPlannerPro to connect their systems, automate repetitive work, and give church administrators more time to serve their communities.

Andrew Schneider · Founder, ChurchPlannerPro

FAQ

Common questions

What is ChurchPlannerPro?

A church management platform that brings members, giving, workflows, forms, calendar, facilities, and reporting into one place — built for the way church administrators actually work.

Who is it for?

Church and parish administrators who are tired of running everything from spreadsheets, email threads, and a stack of disconnected tools.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

No. ChurchPlannerPro integrates with the accounting, giving, and email systems most churches already use, so your existing data flows in. You can keep what already works well for you.
