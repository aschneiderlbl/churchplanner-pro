ChurchPlannerPro Strategic Narrative Framework 1. Brand and Market Context Churches have more software than ever. There are tools for people, giving, groups, events, registrations, volunteers, communications, accounting and nearly every other part of church operations. But adding software has not eliminated the administrative work required to run a church. Staff still maintain data, connect systems, chase volunteers, follow up on forms, reconcile finances, prepare communications and figure out what needs their attention. At the same time, church software has become increasingly complicated. More modules. More configuration. More pricing tiers. More features that many small and midsize churches simply do not need. ChurchPlannerPro is built around a simple promise: Automate your church operations. Everything we do starts there. Instead of simply giving churches more tools to manage their work, ChurchPlannerPro uses software, integrations and automation to reduce the amount of administrative work the church has to do. And we believe churches deserve great software that is affordable and straightforward. Everything you need. Nothing you don’t. One platform. One affordable price. Everything included. The result is less time spent on church operations and more time for what is most important to your church. 2. Ideal Customer Profile ChurchPlannerPro is built primarily for small and midsize churches that do not have large administrative teams. Typically: - Roughly 100–1,000 members - Limited dedicated administrative staff - Pastors, staff or volunteers spending significant time on administrative work - Multiple systems, spreadsheets or manual processes - Repetitive work that happens every week, month or year - Limited time to configure and maintain complicated software - A desire to keep some existing systems while making them work better together - A need for capable software at a price that makes sense for a church - A preference for the software they actually need rather than a large collection of modules and features These churches do not need more software to administer. They need software that helps automate the administration. 3. Brand Promise Automate your church operations. This is the ChurchPlannerPro brand promise. Everything we do should help fulfill it. Every feature should help automate work. Every integration should help automate work. Every workflow should help automate work. Every product decision should ultimately answer: How does this help automate church operations? Automation does not mean removing people from the church. It means removing repetitive administrative work so people have more time for what is most important to their church. 4. Product Philosophy Everything you need. Nothing you don’t. More software is not necessarily better software. ChurchPlannerPro is intentionally built around what small and midsize churches actually need to run their operations. We do not want to build features simply so we can have a longer feature list. We do not want churches navigating dozens of modules they rarely use. And we do not want complexity to become a reason churches need additional staff just to administer their software. ChurchPlannerPro should provide the capabilities churches need, bring those capabilities together into complete workflows and automate as much of the work as possible. That means: - The church operations you actually need - Complete workflows instead of disconnected features - Less configuration - Less complexity - No unnecessary bells and whistles - No paying for functionality you do not need - One affordable price for the entire platform The goal is not to build church software with the most features. The goal is to give churches everything they need, nothing they don’t, and automate as much of the work as possible. 5. Core Belief Church software should do more of the work. The first generation of church software digitized administration. Paper directories became databases. Signup sheets became online forms. Printed schedules became scheduling software. Giving records became digital reports. Those were important improvements. But the church is still responsible for much of the underlying work. Someone still has to update the database. Someone still has to chase the volunteer. Someone still has to follow up on the form. Someone still has to reconcile the financials. Someone still has to notice what fell through the cracks. ChurchPlannerPro believes the next generation of church software should go further. It should automate church operations. 6. Core Customer Tension Churches buy software to reduce administrative work. But too often, software simply changes where the work happens. Instead of maintaining paper records, staff maintain software. Instead of manually creating signup sheets, staff configure forms. Instead of manually creating schedules, staff operate scheduling tools. Instead of reconciling paper reports, staff reconcile digital reports. The work became digital. It did not disappear. Meanwhile, software itself has become another thing churches have to manage. More modules. More configuration. More usage tiers. More pricing decisions. Small and midsize churches are often paying for complexity they do not need while still doing too much of the work themselves. Churches need technology that removes work rather than simply reorganizing it. 7. Villain Administrative work that software should be doing for you. The problem is not technology itself. The problem is stopping at digitization. A volunteer scheduling tool is useful. But the outcome is not having scheduling software. The outcome is having every volunteer shift filled. A giving system is useful. But the outcome is not having another financial dashboard. The outcome is having the financials reconcile every month and accurate giving statements ready on time. A member database is useful. But the outcome is not storing records. The outcome is having accurate information when the church needs it. ChurchPlannerPro is designed around moving from: “Here’s a tool to help you do the work.” to: “ChurchPlannerPro helps handle the work.” 8. Competitive Narrative Church software has gone through its first major transformation. It moved administration from paper and spreadsheets into software. ChurchPlannerPro represents the next transformation: From digitizing church administration to automating church operations. Traditional church software asks: How can software help staff manage this process? ChurchPlannerPro asks: How much of this process can software handle for them? That difference changes how the product is built. Volunteer scheduling is not finished when the church has a scheduling interface. It is finished when the shifts are filled. Sunday school software is not successful because registrations are stored in a database. It is successful when registration, room assignment, attendance, communication and follow-up work together. Financial software is not successful because another report exists. It is successful when the numbers reconcile. And more features do not necessarily create a better product. Small and midsize churches should not have to navigate enterprise-level complexity, configure dozens of modules or pay for capabilities they never use. Everything you need. Nothing you don’t. The next generation of church software should automate the work churches actually need done without creating unnecessary complexity around it. And churches should not have to navigate complicated pricing models to get it. One platform. One affordable price. Everything included. 14.
