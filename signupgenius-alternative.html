From sign-up sheet to volunteer schedule

Church volunteers don't just sign up once.

Every week, your church needs to know:

Who's serving? What's still open? And what changed?

ChurchPlannerPro gives administrators and volunteers a simple workflow for managing it.

1. Admin publishes the schedule

Create the shifts your church needs and assign existing volunteers or leave shifts open.

Publish the schedule when it's ready.

2. Members pick up open shifts

Members can see upcoming opportunities and pick up open shifts themselves.

No spreadsheet. No separate sign-up link to keep track of.

3. Volunteers drop or swap shifts

Plans change.

Instead of every change becoming another email to the church office, volunteers can drop or swap their shifts.

Your schedule stays up to date without your administrator having to coordinate every change manually.

Free comes with ads

One reason SignUpGenius can offer its Basic plan for free is advertising.

On the free plan, ads can appear on the sign-up pages your volunteers visit and in emails sent through SignUpGenius.

Those ads aren't always selected directly by SignUpGenius. Some are supplied automatically by third-party advertising networks. SignUpGenius says it attempts to filter sensitive and controversial categories, but it does not manually review every ad before it's shown.

That means when you send members to volunteer for Sunday morning, your church doesn't completely control what appears alongside your sign-up.

ChurchPlannerPro doesn't put third-party advertising in front of your members.

Your volunteer schedule is part of your church experience, not an advertising platform.

Who should use SignUpGenius?

SignUpGenius is a good option if you primarily need to create a sign-up page and let people choose a time, role, or activity.

It's familiar, works for organizations of all kinds, and its Basic plan is free. The tradeoff with the free plan is that participants may see third-party advertising.

If a standalone sign-up is all your church needs, it may be enough.

ChurchPlannerPro is built for churches that need an actual volunteer scheduling system.
