Looking for a Realm Alternative?

ChurchPlannerPro is a simpler, affordable Realm alternative built for smaller churches that want everything they need without the extra bells and whistles.

Realm is a comprehensive church management platform from ACS Technologies. It gives churches tools for managing people, giving, accounting, groups, events, check-in, communications, volunteers, reporting and more.

That breadth can be valuable for larger or more complex churches.

But not every church needs it.

ChurchPlannerPro is built for smaller churches with lean teams and fewer dedicated administrative or technology staff.

Our goal is simple:

Automate your church operations.

ChurchPlannerPro gives you the tools you need to run your church, automates repetitive administrative work and helps your team spend more time on what is most important to your church.

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

Giving built into both platforms

Both ChurchPlannerPro and Realm support online giving directly within their platforms.

Realm offers Realm Giving, its integrated online giving platform.

ChurchPlannerPro offers built-in giving powered by Stripe.

The bigger difference is what happens around giving.

ChurchPlannerPro helps smaller churches keep giving records accurate, reconcile financials, prepare giving statements and stay on budget without requiring the church to move its entire accounting operation into ChurchPlannerPro.

If your church already uses QuickBooks, you can keep using it.

Realm takes a more comprehensive approach and offers its own optional integrated accounting product.

For churches that want giving and accounting inside the same broader ecosystem, that can be an advantage.

For churches that already have an accounting system they like, ChurchPlannerPro lets you keep it.
